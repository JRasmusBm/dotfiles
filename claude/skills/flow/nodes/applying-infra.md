# Applying infra by hand

- What changes, why now, and what plan do I expect (the `Plan:`
  line, the resources)? How long does it take, and how do we revert
  it?
- Is the plan from the latest base, or would it undo someone else's
  apply?
- Will what Rasmus pastes run exactly what was planned (the
  targets, one line, a saved plan)?
- Does the plan log match what I expected, before he applies? If
  not, throw the plan away and say why.
- During the apply: what am I watching to know it worked?
