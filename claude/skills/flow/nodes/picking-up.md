# Picking up a ticket

- What is the problem, who has it, and why now? Can I say it in my
  own words, with the numbers?
- Where are we going: the team's priorities, the project, recent
  Slack discussion? In what way does this ticket move us there, and
  would that change how I solve it?
- What am I assuming? Which of those can I check now (the code, the
  data, the thread, the asker)?
- Is there a suggested solution: in the ticket, the Slack thread, a
  comment? Whose words are they? The asker's or a stakeholder's carry
  real weight: what made them suggest it? What does it get right,
  what would I change, is there something better?
- Does it need Rasmus's design direction? Would his answer throw away
  what I'd build, or can I build the cheapest version and show him?
- Is someone else already on it: a teammate, another agent, an open
  PR? Is it claimed where teammates will see it?
- Is it still relevant? Did anything change since it was written?
