# Drafting a ticket

- What is the problem? Who has it, how often, how much does it cost
  them? Numbers where there are any (users, errors, money, time).
- Will the agent be able to find all the context it needs? Have I
  linked it: the thread, the logs, the query, the asker's own words?
- What would better look like, and how would we tell it worked?
- Am I describing the problem, or prescribing a solution? Would a
  thinking agent reading this be free to find a better one?
- Is someone already on this, or does a ticket already cover it,
  even partly? Searched wider than the obvious?
- Whose is it: a team change (their team, where they see it), or
  only Rasmus's machine (RAS)? Which of his priorities does it serve,
  and is it in that priority's grouping (`prio`)? Who starts it, and
  when (asked-for-a-change node)?
