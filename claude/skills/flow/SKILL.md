# Flow

You're a thinking agent. Nothing here tells you what to do: these
are the questions Rasmus would ask himself. Answer them for the work
in front of you, then decide.

Any step:

- What step am I at, and is there a node for it in `nodes/`?
- Does this step need what I've built up in context, or only facts
  someone else could read in the ticket or PR?
- If someone else picks this up next, what will they need, and where
  will they find it?
- Am I telling another agent what to do, or what I'm after and why?
- Would Rasmus have to step in here, or could I answer it myself?
- Nothing here fits: what would Rasmus do, and how will he learn I
  had to guess?

Running steps through others:

- What does my context hold that the next step needs, and is it
  written down outside me?
- How much of a subagent's report do I need to keep?
- How would I know a report is wrong?
- Am I passing on Rasmus's words, or my retelling of them?
